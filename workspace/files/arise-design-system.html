Vision · 2026

The Year of
Uncommon Favor

Favor that cannot be explained by human effort, connections, or circumstance — favor that comes solely from the hand of a good, generous God. Doors long closed will swing open. "For You, O Lord, will bless the righteous; with favor You will surround him as with a shield." (Psalm 5:12)

Mission · 2026

Impact Tioga

To bring the life-changing love of Jesus Christ into every corner of our community. To help Tioga discover Christ, to build the community together with neighbors, schools, businesses and local leaders, and to support one another in prayer, encouragement, and practical help.

Purpose

Character & Compassion

To produce disciples who confidently display the character of our God while compassionately sharing the love of Jesus with others — men, women, and young people rooted in God's Word, transformed by His Spirit, reflecting His nature in every area of life. We share the Gospel not out of duty but out of overflowing compassion: meeting people where they are, listening to their stories, serving their needs.
