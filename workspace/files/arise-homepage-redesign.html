What To Expect

Parking

Park wherever there's room. Our parking ushers are out on Sunday mornings — follow their direction and they'll get you where you need to be.

What to wear

There's no dress code. You'll see jeans and you'll see suits. Come as you are — we mean it.

When to arrive

Come a little early your first time. Room to park without rushing, find a seat you like, and meet somebody before service starts.
